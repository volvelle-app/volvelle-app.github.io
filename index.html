Scroll your own notes.

Next time you reach for your phone to scroll, scroll your own notes instead. Volvelle turns your Apple Notes into a deck you browse by swiping up, and the ideas, plans and lists you saved come back on their own.

Your notes stay on your iPhone. No account, no ads, no tracking.
